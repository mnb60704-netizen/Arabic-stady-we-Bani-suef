## 2025-05-18 - Keyboard Navigation for Card-Based Grids in Vanilla HTML SPAs
**Learning:** In vanilla HTML single-page applications where card grids serve as primary navigation links, cards often use generic `<div>` containers with only mouse `onclick` handlers. Without semantic roles, focusability (`tabindex="0"`), accessible labels (`aria-label`), keyboard handlers (`Enter`/`Space`), and visible `:focus-visible` styles, these elements are completely invisible and unusable to screen reader and keyboard-only users.
**Action:** When working on vanilla HTML/JS card grids, ensure cards have `role="button"` (or `link`), `tabindex="0"`, descriptive `aria-label`, visible `:focus-visible` ring matching the theme's accent color, and delegated keyboard activation for `Enter` and `Space`.
